# Cards expand in place to reveal their details

A collapsed card shows its title and badges. Clicking its face expands it in place to show its objective, acceptance criteria, and child checklist. Tapping the face again collapses it. Only one card expands at a time. Its title then opens the work item, while a modifier-click opens it directly without expanding.

Keeping the expanded details in the board preserves the parent's context and supports work on a child checklist without navigating away. The expansion reflows the column, so a side panel remains a possible replacement if that becomes too disruptive.

## Considered options

A modal or side panel avoids reflow but separates the details from the board. Showing the full body inline adds more content than needed for this quick view.
